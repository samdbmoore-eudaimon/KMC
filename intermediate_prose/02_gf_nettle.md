# gf_nettle — "The Pattern in the Fog-Glass"

**tagline**
Kept in from the cold by a cough that won't quit, Nettle finds the Engine's fog keeps better time than anyone in Ferrow Lane has noticed.

---

**intro**
Nettle was meant to be asleep. Mother Halloway had said so twice, once at supper and once again with the candle held close to check the cough hadn't come back sharp, and both times Nettle had nodded and meant it, mostly. But the room was cold in the particular way that told her the Engine was about to knock, and there was a stretch of bare glass above her bed that no one else in the house ever bothered to watch. So she got up on her knees on the cold boards, breathed a small patch of warmth onto the pane, and waited. Down at the end of Ferrow Lane the outfall grate gave its first low knock, felt more than heard, right through the floor and the bedframe and her own chest. Four times a day the Turn breathed its fog up through that grate, and four times a day everyone else in the lane simply waited it out. Nettle had started counting instead, weeks ago, chalking the knocks on the underside of her sill where Mother Halloway's candle never reached. Tonight the knocks felt sharper than usual, more countable, as though the Engine wanted, just this once, to be understood.
