**tagline**
A strongbox has gone missing from Ashcombe Yard, a runner boy is taking the blame for it, and Delphine Ashworth has never in her life let a good scandal go uninvestigated.

**intro**
Delphine Ashworth was, by her own cheerful estimate, the best-informed officer at Ashcombe Yard, and she had the duty room's full attention to prove it. She was mid-anecdote about a Reeve's daughter and a borrowed hat when the sergeant came in at a pace that meant something had actually happened, which was rare enough to be worth noticing on its own.

The counting room strongbox had gone in the night. Forty-odd grist in collected conduct fines, gone clean out of a locked room with one officer meant to be watching the corridor outside it. Standing already accused, wedged between two constables twice his size, was a Under runner boy called Fossey, who delivered post along that corridor most evenings and had, unhelpfully for himself, been seen doing exactly that the evening before.

'Convenient,' murmured Delphine, not quite quietly enough.

Across the room, Corvain, three years her senior and twice as tired, raised one eyebrow at her without looking up from her own paperwork. 'Something to add, Ashworth, or just enjoying the view?'

'Both,' said Delphine, and drifted, with the unhurried confidence of someone who has never once doubted she was allowed to be somewhere, over to the roster pinned by the door.
