# gf_tambrindle — "The Vent-Count Watch"

**tagline**
A rush job across the seam, a rival porter's dare, and forty counts before the fog comes down. Tam only has to be quicker than usual.

**intro**
Tam heard it through the floorboards before anyone said it out loud: Ma Halloway at the table with the rent docket and the chalk tab from Marta's laid side by side, not adding up, going quiet in the particular way she went quiet when she was doing sums she didn't like the answer to.

Three marks short. Maybe three and a half. Nobody had said it to him. Nobody needed to.

He was supposed to be mending the barrow wheel, and he was, technically, in that he had a knife out and a peg half whittled and had been for two days, because the knife kept finding better things to do than the peg. He put it away, laced his boots twice each, and went to see what the yard had going spare that morning. Portering paid by the load and the hour, and today he intended to be the fastest thing on the seam.
