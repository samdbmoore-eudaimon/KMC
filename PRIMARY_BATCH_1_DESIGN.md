# Primary Batch 1 design

## Scope

1. Negative Numbers
2. Place Value
3. Rounding & Estimating
4. Addition & Subtraction
5. Mental Maths Shortcuts

## Design rule

Existing sound mathematical structures are retained. Context variation is added inside stable families rather than by multiplying small registries. All five topics will emit `structureId`, `variantId`, `representation` and optional `stretch` metadata. Lesson examples will link to representative `structureId` values.

## Difficulty progression

| Band | Reading and reasoning demand |
|---|---|
| D1 | One direct idea, friendly values, explicit unknown |
| D2 | One extra inference, changed unknown or less familiar representation |
| D3 | Two or more linked steps, selection of relevant information or reverse reasoning |
| D4 | Unfamiliar transfer, indirect information, justification, reconstruction or legitimate stretch |

## Topic designs

### Place Value

- Retain the existing composing, partitioning, comparing, ordering, digit-movement and reconstruction families.
- Treat counting through zero as intentional prerequisite transfer from the earlier Negative Numbers lesson.
- Add Roman numeral reading and construction during the batch if it can be introduced without displacing the stronger existing families.
- Context rotation: orchard stores, row numbers, harvest tallies, map distances and token values.
- Main representations: place-value tables, expanded form, digit cards, direct notation and short Ninefold Orchard stories.

### Rounding & Estimating

- Retain direct rounding, number-line proximity, halfway, reverse bounds, estimation and contextual rounding decisions.
- Context rotation: harvest counts, travel distances, crowd estimates, storage capacity and market budgets.
- D1 asks for nearest friendly values; D2 changes place or representation; D3 estimates operations and reverses intervals; D4 uses bounds and contextual decisions.

### Negative Numbers

- Retain number-line, ordering, temperature, floor, interval, sequence and compound-change families.
- New lesson teaches these families before Place Value reuses counting through zero.
- Context rotation: winter orchard temperatures, cellar floors, account balances, height above/below the mill stream and score changes.
- D1 identifies and moves; D2 crosses zero; D3 combines changes; D4 reconstructs or compares compound changes.

### Addition & Subtraction

Use five stable families at every difficulty:

| Family | Core route | Rotating settings | Development |
|---|---|---|---|
| `remaining_amount` | whole minus used | match time, journey, orchard stock, water, score target | direct remainder to multi-stage and reverse starting amount |
| `combine_totals` | parts make a whole | baskets, visitors, harvest rows, coins, books | two parts to several changing parts and missing original group |
| `difference_compare` | compare two quantities | heights, scores, distances, stores, collected fruit | direct gap to changed quantities and sum/difference reconstruction |
| `number_line_change` | model signed movement | path, temperature, cellar lift, stream height, score | one move to crossing zero, compound movement and missing start |
| `calculation_detective` | inverse/error/unknown reasoning | direct notation with Ninefold character framing | missing part to inverse choice, error diagnosis and missing digit |

Expected representation mixture: roughly half stories, one third diagrams and one sixth direct questions over a large sample.

### Mental Maths Shortcuts

- Retain addition, subtraction, multiplication and division compensation, recognition, reversal and error spotting.
- Add contextual variants without hiding the shortcut: market totals, paired harvest crates, equal journey gaps, packet scaling and shared work.
- D1 chooses or applies a friendly adjustment; D2 selects a valid transformation; D3 handles decimals and multiple operations; D4 reverses or diagnoses a faulty shortcut.

## Lesson coverage

- Place Value, Rounding and Mental Maths retain their sound prose and 4-example sections; examples receive representative family links.
- Negative Numbers receives a complete lesson before it is used as a prerequisite.
- Addition & Subtraction receives a complete lesson with four progressively demanding examples per section.
- Every worked solution states what is known, the plan, each calculation and a check or interpretation.

## Acceptance evidence

- Five eligible families at D1-D4 for Addition & Subtraction.
- Existing minimum family counts retained for the other four topics.
- No placeholders in Batch 1 lessons.
- Every Batch 1 lesson example links to a valid family.
- Batch 1 questions emit variant and representation metadata.
- Editorial samples cover every family and difficulty.
- Primary sanity, cross-module sanity and rebuild checks pass.
