# Intermediate Adventures: Prose Review Brief

## Task for the Writer AI

Below are all prose snippets from the fourteen existing Gifford intermediate adventures. The prose was written by an AI and is generally serviceable but uneven. Your task is to **review and rewrite** every snippet.

### What to fix

- **Forced maths references**: Remove lines where the character reflects on arithmetic for its own sake — "the arithmetic of it being tidy", "numbers she could finally trust", "doing the sum in his head" used purely as a mood-setter. The maths in these adventures is in the puzzle scenes; the story scenes should be about the characters and their world.
- **Overlong sentences**: Break up sentences that pile clause upon clause. Aim for rhythm: short, medium, long, varied.
- **Flat or generic phrasing**: Cut filler phrases ("in a way that had never quite let go", "which was its own kind of knowing"). Replace with something concrete and specific to the character.
- **Throat-clearing openings**: Many scenes open with a sentence that just restates setting. Cut it or replace with action.

### What to keep

- **UK English** throughout. No z-spellings, no Oxford commas.
- **No em-dashes**. Use commas, colons, semicolons or new sentences instead.
- **Voice**: Pratchett/Tiffany Aching register. Dry, affectionate, grounded. Treats children and working people with dignity. Humour comes from precision, not whimsy.
- **World**: The Under is an industrial underworld lit by gas and fog. The Turn is a giant engine that vents scalding fog four times a day. The Rise is the wealthy upper city. Wrought are upper class, Rustbound are working class, Sirrath are a tiger-like non-human people, Laachmi have horns. Keep all world details exactly as written.
- **All puzzle text, question/answer/hint/solution fields and dice predict fields are excluded below** — do not rewrite anything you don't see here.

### Format for your return

Return an MD file in exactly the same structure as this one. Same section headings, same snippet labels. Replace only the quoted text inside each snippet. Do not add or remove snippets. Do not reorder them.

Approximate length: keep each rewritten snippet within 20% of the original word count unless the original is genuinely overlong.

## 3. gf_higgins — "The Seven and a Half Rule"

**Tagline**
> "Sump Gate Seven vents four times a day, on the tick, without exception. Corporal Higgins has never once needed to wonder what an exception would look like."

**intro**
> "Sump Gate Seven sat where Tallow Street stopped pretending the Rise and the Under were the same city, and admitted it outright. In six years he had never once been late for the click.\n\nThe click came first, a knock felt up through the boots, the Turn clearing its throat before it spoke. Higgins had read the Vent Order so many times he could recite it walking backwards in the dark, which he had in fact done once, for a bet he did not remember losing, to a colleague he did not much like.\n\n'Correct in every particular,' his old training sergeant used to say, tapping the card, 'and you will never once have to decide anything for yourself.'
