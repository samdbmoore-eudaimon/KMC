# Primary Batch 2 design

## Scope

6. Times Tables
7. Factors & Multiples
8. Column Multiplication
9. Division & Remainders
10. Long Division

## Existing strengths to preserve

All five generators already expose at least five genuinely different families at every difficulty. Their lesson sections already contain four complete worked examples. The batch therefore preserves the sound mathematics and focuses on the missing relationship between lessons, generators, verbal settings and visual representations.

## Progression and context plan

| Topic | D1 to D4 development | Main reasoning routes | Ninefold settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Times Tables | recall and missing factors to partitioning, scaling, reversal and comparison | arrays, equal groups, fact families, distributive reasoning, reverse scaling | orchard rows, seed trays, market bundles, fruit crates, tally boards |
| Factors & Multiples | recognise examples to HCF/LCM, factor products and prime decomposition | divisibility, grouping, rectangular arrays, synchronised cycles, decomposition | bell cycles, gate patterns, planting plots, basket groups, seed packets |
| Column Multiplication | one-digit products and carrying to full long multiplication, diagnosis and compound area | place value, partial products, estimation, comparison, error analysis | store ledgers, field areas, cart journeys, unit prices, stacked crates |
| Division & Remainders | quotient/remainder identification to interpretation, reconstruction and chained remainders | grouping, leftover meaning, rounding up/down, fraction/decimal remainder, inverse checking | transport, packing, market money, shared harvest, storage shelves |
| Long Division | short division and procedural steps to two-digit divisors, remainder forms and error diagnosis | place-value division, ratio tables, estimation, carried remainders, inverse checking | orchard stores, mill deliveries, seed sharing, path sections, market orders |

## Acceptance evidence

- Each difficulty retains at least five reachable families.
- Every generated question emits `structureId`, `variantId` and `representation`.
- Large samples remain within the practical 40-70% story, 20-45% diagram and 8-30% direct tolerances.
- One live sample from every family and difficulty is written to an editorial evidence report.
- Primary, Junior and Intermediate sanity suites and the app rebuild pass.
