# Wallaby Maths Expeditions roster

> Central entry point: `EXPEDITIONS_MASTER_BRIEF.md`. Use this roster with
> `EXPEDITIONS_CARD_COMBAT_CONTRACT.md` and `EXPEDITIONS_CARD_AUDIT.md` for card capability.

This is the human-readable snapshot of the assignments in `expeditions/expedition-roster.js`. Existing Adventure roles are preserved. Remaining roles are assigned from the printed statistics and balanced to six of each role per module.

Key: Mv is movement per action, Rg is basic range and HP is hearts.
