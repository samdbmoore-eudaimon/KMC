## Causes (reproduced from Sam's retained trace, `evidence/playthrough-recovery-20261003/sam-playthrough-trace.json`)

Regression: `expeditions/v2/recovery.test.js` (replays that trace through the production reducer).

1. **Sorrel and the lower bridge: a map defect, not a rule.** Sorrel stood on permanent bridge `3,6`. Its east neighbour `4,6` was a decorative hedge and its other eastern neighbours were water, so the bridge was a dead end: from `3,6` the engine offers only hexes back on the west bank. The cause is hedge placement in the certified tier 1 river seed 1 map. No AP, holder, root or closed-passage effect is involved.
2. **Tuppence beside the controlled bridge: the bridge was raised.** Tuppence stood on `3,4`, next to the puzzle-controlled bridge `4,4`. The puzzle had not been activated, so `passages["4,4"].open` was false and no output was powered; the engine correctly refuses to enter it, and `5,4` is not reachable until the number lock is activated (the same hero reaches `5,4` once the passage is open). The deck looked like a bridge with nothing explaining why it did not work, so this is a feedback defect. The map renderer should draw a raised bridge visibly raised (Codex-owned `MapViewport`/`RiverTerrain`); I added the explanation in the interface (below).
