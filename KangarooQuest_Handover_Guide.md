## 2. What this project is

A single-file HTML/React educational game for two children (9 and 10) preparing for the UK Junior Maths Challenge (Years 7–9 content, stretching to Intermediate Maths Challenge standard). Built for local use in a plain browser; also runs inside Claude.ai where `window.storage` exists (outside it, a localStorage shim in the HTML shell takes over). The game is fully offline: there are no AI/network features. All teaching is static (lessons, worked solutions, worked examples) and all marking is computed locally.

**The core loop:** answer questions → earn stars (more at higher levels) → 20 stars = 1 card pack → unique cards build **strength** → strength + a passed **level exam** unlock the **boss fight** → beating the boss unlocks the next level and the next **story chapter**.

## 3. Current feature state (all built, tested, shipped)

- **10 quest levels** in Practice; levels 1–4 map to legacy difficulty tiers d1–d4; from level 5 the five deep generators mix in with rising probability (≈20% → 85%), scaling internally with level.
- **Five deep multi-step generators** (`DEEP_TOPICS`): epicJourney 🗺️, moneyTrail 💰, digitDetective 🕵️, numberMachine ⚙️, pythagQuest 📐 (Triangle Trials — ramps to IMC standard: 3D cuboid diagonals, shared-height wire problems, disguised-triple algebra).
- **10 bosses** (`BOSSES`, thresholds 4/8/13/18/26/34/50/68/90/105 vs max collection strength **134** — 122 from base cards plus 12 from adventure upgrades; the easing was accepted deliberately since upgrades are earned). Boss n gates level n+1.
- **Two-condition boss gate:** card strength **AND** a passed level exam — a full 25-question mock sat at that level with at least the level's pass mark correct. The pass mark **scales per level** (`EXAM_PASS_MARKS` map + `examPassMarkFor(lv)`): 12/15/18/21 across each paper band, i.e. L1–4 = 12/15/18/21, L5–8 = 12/15/18/21, L9–10 = 12/15. Paper mapping (`examKindFor`) was **re-banded to align with those marks**: levels 1–4 JMC (`"jmc"`), 5–8 Junior Kangaroo (`"kangaroo"`), 9–10 Year 9 stretch (`"year9"`). Wrong paper never counts. Passes stored per level in `progress.mocks` (a boolean, so profiles that passed under old rules stay passed). Changed from a flat `EXAM_PASS_MARK = 20` and 1–3/4–7/8–10 banding to make early gates gentler and keep late gates biting.
- **5-tier rarity** with strength: common 1, uncommon 2, rare 4, epic 8, legendary 16. Duplicates add nothing. **Commons can be upgraded to uncommon (strength 2) via the Adventures system** — `collectionStrength` counts a common as `RARITY.uncommon.str` when `isUpgraded(progress, id)` (all 3 of its adventure items collected).
- **Adventures (D&D-style party quests, v2 with combat).** New Home tile (screen `"adventures"`). Each of the 12 commons has a class (`CARD_CLASS`: tank cubble/pebble/squarby, healer addy/frostcal, wizard countra/loopy, melee tritip/sparkfin, ranged hunchik/zoomby/burrowl) with a once-per-adventure signature move (`CLASSES`), now context-aware: tank Shield Wall = failed roll counts as success OR blocks an enemy strike; healer Mend = wrong puzzle back to good path OR restores 2 hearts in combat; wizard Reveal = free hint on puzzles or combat questions; melee Strike Again = reroll any die including a missed to-hit; ranged Scout Ahead / Spot a Weakness = preview choice outcomes OR drop an enemy's to-hit target by 2. Player picks the mandatory hero plus 2 owned companions; party composition is a real tactical decision.
- **Combat system.** Scene type `combat`: enemy `{name, emoji, hits (2-5), toHit (d20 target), hitText, strikeText, defeatText}` plus scene-level `qLevel`. Round loop: roll the d20 to hit; a landed hit generates a quick-fire maths question live from the game's own generators (`makeLevelQuestion(qLevel)`, deduped per run via a `seenQ` ref) and a correct answer deals 1 damage — a natural 20 plus a correct answer is a CRITICAL for 2. A missed roll or wrong answer triggers an enemy strike: the player chooses Shield Wall (tank), reroll (melee, roll-misses only) or "Take the hit (−1 ❤️)". `window.__kqAdvQA` exposes the correct index as a test hook. Combat options row has className `kq-combat-options` for tests.
- **Party hearts.** The party shares 3 hearts across the whole adventure, shown in the party bar (pips carry `title="{h} of 3 hearts"` — tests assert on this). Zero hearts is never a death: a "beaten" panel explains the party retreats, the run drops to `mid` quality, hearts are patched to 2 and play resumes at the combat's `retreat` scene. Hearts can be restored by healer Mend (+2, cap 3) and scene-level `bonusHearts` (night road reward, +1).
- **Scene engine (`AdventureRun`)** also supports: `story` (optional `grantItem`/`grantComponent`/`setMid`/`bonusStars`/`bonusHearts`), `choice` (options with Scout previews), `skillcheck` (`stat` index into `STAT_DEFS` [Arithmetic, Geometry, Logic, Science, Speed] + `dc`; the player picks which party member attempts it, seeing each member's stat value from `adventureCardView(m, progress).s` including item boosts; roll d20 + stat vs dc; on failure Shield Wall counts as success, melee can reroll, or accept the `fail` branch), `dice` (predict-the-probability question first — correct prediction gives +1 to the roll; animated `DiceRoll`, advantage = roll twice keep higher), `puzzle` (hard static JMC-level MC; wrong answers show the full worked solution then take the mid branch, never a hard fail), `forge` (needs all run-local components) and `end` (good/mid text, stars 6 good / 4 mid + any bonus, grants the quest item). Item structure per hero: **all items gated** — item 1 won by an early skill check (failing it loses the item for that run, driving replay), item 2 = completion reward (any ending), item 3 forged from components scattered across branches. State: `progress.cardItems` (persisted immediately and idempotently) and `progress.adventures` (`{done, best}`). `adventureCardView` shows item stat boosts and displays fully upgraded commons as uncommon; CardsScreen shows item pips and an upgrade banner. **All 12 adventures now shipped, every one 51 scenes / 3 acts to the same v3 shape.** One per common: `addy` ("The Ledger of Lost Sums"), `cubble` ("The Last True Corner", tank/Geometry), `countra` ("The Sums That Bred", wizard/Arithmetic), `zoomby` ("The Day the Hive Ran Slow", ranged/Speed), `pebble` ("The Wall That Wouldn't", tank/Geometry), `squarby` ("The Tiling That Came Undone", tank/Geometry), `frostcal` ("The Melting of the Measures", healer/Arithmetic), `loopy` ("The Argument That Broke", wizard/Logic), `tritip` ("The City That Lost Its Bearings", melee/Geometry), `sparkfin` ("The Stilling of the Tides", melee/Science), `hunchik` ("The Case of the Missing Because", ranged/Logic) and `burrowl` ("The Warren That Wouldn't Add Up", ranged/Arithmetic). No "coming soon" remains.
- **Addy's adventure v3 shape (use as the template for the batch):** 47 scenes in 3 acts of expanded Pratchett/Tiffany-Aching prose, with **3 stat-based skill checks spanning 3 different stats** so party composition matters. Act 1: intro → skill check gating item 1 (glint_check, Arithmetic dc 10; fail = item lost this run, recoverable only by replay) → tutorial combat (2 hits, toHit 8, qLevel 2) → a 3-way route split (Market/Bridge/Fen) where each route earns the SAME first component through a different challenge type (hard puzzle / dice set-piece / combat) so one skilled run can still collect everything. Act 2: converge → dice set-piece (component 2) → skill check with a reward (tinker_check, Logic dc 11; success = +2 bonusStars, fail = no penalty) → risk-reward camp choice (night road: d20 advantage, success = +3 bonusStars +1 bonusHearts) → mid combat (3 hits, toHit 9, qLevel 3) → hard puzzle. Act 3: arrival → skill check with a cost (page_check, Speed dc 12; fail = setMid) → hard puzzle (component 3) → mini-boss combat (4 hits, toHit 10, qLevel 3) → forge → finale → end. Vary the check stakes like this (gate an item / grant a bonus / risk the run quality) and vary the stats so different companions shine. Every failure branch sets `setMid: true` for consistency; NEVER put `setMid` on an `end` scene (quality is read at entry). Combat retreat scenes are plain stories — the engine applies mid + heart patching itself.
- **Level-scaled pack odds** (`packWeights(lv)`): L1–2 common 100%; L3–4 uncommon 55%; L5–6 rare 50%; L7–8 epic 45%; L9–10 legendary 40% (commons fade to 6%). Plus **one duplicate reroll per pack** in `rollCard(lv, ownedCards)`. Simulated: ~37 packs median for the whole game (was ~174 under flat odds).
- **11-chapter story** embedded (`STORY`, ~10,600 words, Pratchett/Tiffany-Aching voice, *The Kangaroo Quest and the Coming of the Muddle*). Chapter n+1 unlocks on beating boss n; delivered as post-victory popup AND saved to the Storybook screen.
- **All 10 boss portraits** embedded in `BOSS_ART` (640px JPEGs, ~100–155KB each). `BossPortrait` component used on Home banner (128px), Practice banner (92px), boss modal (170–180px), Storybook tiles, and the Mock Test "Gatekeeper" banner (92px). Clicking a portrait opens that boss's chapter (`openBossChapter` in App's `shared`). Story reader and Mock Test screens carry the art as headers/backgrounds.
- **Teaching layer:** all 41 topics have lessons (`CONCEPTS`: idea/method/tip), 19 with "⚠️ Watch out" misconception warnings sourced from the NCETM KS3 mastery materials. Olympiad screen has a collapsible **Training camp** (`OLYMPIAD_PLAYBOOK`: Understand/Plan/Do/Check + 8 named tactics) and **16 problems** (`OLYMPIAD`), each with a hidden tactic-hint button and a full checked model solution. Every practice generator returns a `solution` array.
- **Mock papers ramp** with tower level (`buildPaper(kind, towerLevel)`): deep questions mix into the tail from level 5; base difficulty shifts up a tier from level 8.
- **Academy** unlocks at level 5; **Olympiad** tile at level 9.
- **Progress reset (v3):** storage keys are `kq_profiles_v3`, `kq_current_v3`, `kq_prof3_<id>`. Old v1 keys are deliberately ignored (legacy migration removed) — this build forced fresh accounts. Old data still sits untouched under v1 keys if rollback is ever wanted.
- `freshProgress()` includes `unlockedLevel: 1, bossesBeaten: 0, mocks: {}, testHistory: [], cardItems: {}, adventures: {}`; profile load merges `{...freshProgress(), ...raw}` so new fields are always safe to add.

```bash
# entry.jsx renders <App/> into #root. app.jsx imports ./kq-art.js and ./kq-content.js;
# esbuild follows those imports automatically and inlines the art back into the bundle.
cat > entry.jsx <<'EOF'
import React from "react";
import { createRoot } from "react-dom/client";
import App from "./app.jsx";
createRoot(document.getElementById("root")).render(React.createElement(App));
EOF
npx esbuild entry.jsx --bundle --minify --define:process.env.NODE_ENV='"production"' --outfile=bundle.min.js

# assemble the single-file HTML: splice the fresh bundle into the shell from source.html
python3 - <<'EOF'
import re, pathlib
shell = pathlib.Path("source.html").read_text(); bundle = pathlib.Path("bundle.min.js").read_text()
m = re.search(r'\(\(\)=>\{var', shell); s = shell.rfind("<script>", 0, m.start()); e = shell.find("</script>", m.end())
pathlib.Path("KangarooMathsQuest.html").write_text(shell[:s+len("<script>")] + bundle + shell[e:])
EOF
```

## 7. THE SIX IMPROVEMENTS TO BUILD (agreed backlog)

**1. Duplicate cards → stars. ✅ SHIPPED.** After the one reroll, a duplicate pull auto-converts to stars: `DUPLICATE_STARS = { common: 2, uncommon: 2, rare: 3, epic: 4, legendary: 5 }` (search anchor, sits right after `RARITY_ORDER`). `openPack` in CardsScreen computes `dupBonus` before updating state, folds it into `progress.stars` with the same overflow-into-a-new-pack arithmetic used elsewhere (`if (stars >= PACK_COST) { packs += Math.floor(stars/PACK_COST); stars %= PACK_COST; }`), and still increments the duplicate's card count so "You own N of this card" stays accurate. The reveal popup shows a teal "Duplicate! +N ⭐" pill in place of the gold "NEW CREATURE!" pill when `!reveal.isNew`. Verified with a star-conservation math test (100 checks across all rarities × all starting star counts) and two jsdom mount tests driving the real component tree end to end (forced-duplicate pull and forced-new-card pull, both against the assembled HTML as well as the source jsx).

**2. Weak-spot training. ✅ SHIPPED.** Practice (practice mode only, not Learn) has a "Train my weak spots" toggle under the level picker. Verified with a 20-check jsdom mount test driving the real component tree (export round-trip, a distinct-profile import + restore, and an invalid-file error path) against both the source jsx and the assembled HTML.

  - `magicGrid` (Magic Grids): shared-total lines (crosses, rows, opposite pairs), full 3×3 magic squares (built by a verified linear transform of the Lo Shu square, so every random grid is guaranteed valid), sourcing the shared constant from a row/column/diagonal/grand-total, two-step chains, and the centre-symmetry property (opposite cells sum to 2×centre).
  - `shapeFold` (Cuts & Folds): fold-and-punch hole counts (2^n layers, k punches → k×2^n holes, and their reverses), the classic pieces-from-n-straight-cuts formula 1+n+n(n-1)/2 (verified against the lazy-caterer sequence) and its incremental/reverse forms, and equal-area cuts via additive/subtractive rectangle decomposition.
  - `gridLogic` (Logic Grids): 3×3 Latin squares and 4×4 mini-sudoku (built from a verified base grid + digit relabelling permutation), row/column/box elimination combined in every order, two-blank solve chains, counting remaining candidates mid-elimination, and the grand-total-is-40 shortcut for a 4×4 grid.
  - `networkGraph` (Networks): the handshake formula n(n-1)/2 and its reverse, minimum links to join k separate groups (proven group-size is a red herring — only the group count matters), chromatic number of a cycle (2 if even, 3 if odd) vs a complete graph (always n), and edge-resilience (a cycle needs 2 cuts to disconnect, a path only 1).
  - `spatialTransform` (Nets & Symmetry): regular-polygon lines of symmetry and rotational order (both equal the side count — but explicitly NOT for irregular shapes, which get their own verified lookup table), cube-net opposite-face folding (a verified Latin-cross net topology: the 1st/3rd and 2nd/4th of a row-of-four pair up, the perpendicular arms pair with each other), and rotation-vs-reflection chirality (a rotation preserves clockwise reading order around a cube corner; only a mirror image reverses it).
- **Verification approach for all 6:** every generator was first written and fuzz-tested standalone in a scratchpad (2,000-3,600 samples each) before touching the real source files — checking structural validity (5 distinct options, valid correctIndex, svg present) plus independent re-derivation of the underlying maths fact (e.g. the magic-square construction was checked against real row/column/diagonal sums across 2,000 samples; the cuts formula against the known lazy-caterer sequence; the cube-net topology and chirality logic against hand-verified cases).
